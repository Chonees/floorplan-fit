# MVP UX refreshed for CAD-faithful pinch curation

## What

Updated `MVP-UX.md` so the product UX source reflects the current branch truth.

## Why

The previous MVP UX still described an accept-first curated-wall flow with `stable_wall_id`, `locked/flexible/stretchable`, merge/split, and wall metadata as the main curation surface. The active direction is now CAD-faithful curation with separated artifacts and strategic pinch groups/markers.

## Where

- `MVP-UX.md`
- `obsidian-vault/Current State.md`

## Learned

- Loop 1 UX should teach admins to audit CAD artifacts and authorize future shrink zones, not to redraw or accept every wall manually.
- Loop 2 UX should explain fit proposals in terms of allowed pinch groups, protected artifacts, and measurable impact.
- Dimensions remain a future artifact family and must be described as recalculable/auditable, not decorative text.
