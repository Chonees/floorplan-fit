# Point.ai manual includes the documented research pause

## What

Appended an exclusive final page to `Point.ai-system-manual.pdf`:

`Why Development Was Paused - The Research Boundary`

The page explains:

- the domain shift, mixed-style training, corrupted-input, metric-divergence, and geometry-consistency problems identified;
- why raster segmentation did not provide authoritative CAD topology;
- why continuation required wall/opening models, a typed wall graph, a global solver, an exact renderer, CAD validation, representative benchmarks, and roughly 200-500 proprietary gold vector plans;
- why a credible demo needed to prove the complete editable-CAD contract rather than only produce an attractive preview or openable DXF;
- that the research/time tradeoff was discussed with Carlos before work continued on FloorplanFit, without attributing the work to a team;
- that Point.ai remains documented, viable, and technically resumable.

The final wording states the research boundary directly and removes defensive declarations about surrender, abandonment, or the developer's ability to continue.
