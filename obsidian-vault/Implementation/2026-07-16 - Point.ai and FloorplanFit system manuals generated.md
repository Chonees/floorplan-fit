## Point.ai blocker retained

The Point.ai manual preserves the product blocker:

- segmentation does not guarantee endpoints, continuity, closed loops, junction identity, wall thickness, opening ownership, or editable CAD topology;
- a production-oriented path requires typed wall and opening models, a typed wall graph, a global geometric/topological solver, an exact DXF renderer, CAD validation, representative benchmarks, and roughly 200-500 proprietary gold vector plans;
- FloorplanFit reduces the critical-path risk by making managed DXF the source of truth, persisting human-reviewed curation, and applying deterministic changes only inside authorized zones.
