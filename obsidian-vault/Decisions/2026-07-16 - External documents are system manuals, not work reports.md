# External documents are system manuals, not work reports

## Decision

The primary external documents for Point.ai and FloorplanFit are English system architecture and operations manuals.

They document:

- system purpose and authority boundaries;
- architecture and subsystem responsibilities;
- data structures and persistence;
- operational workflows and control points;
- technology stack;
- implemented capabilities, safeguards, limitations, and extension strategy;
- the Point.ai structured-CAD blocker and its strategic relationship to FloorplanFit.

## Exclusions

The manuals do not describe how the documentation was assembled and do not contain:

- time tracking or work-hour totals;
- daily chronology, dates, durations, or project-age calculations;
- source-control history;
- note-taking or evidence-gathering methodology;
- internal reporting mechanics.

Those records remain separate from the system manuals.

## Editorial model

The manuals use a disciplined strategic structure:

- establish the source of truth before describing mechanisms;
- present capabilities before constraints;
- expose control surfaces, dependencies, and failure boundaries;
- distinguish authoritative behavior from optional assistance;
- preserve extension options without promising unimplemented scope;
- remove process noise and unsupported marketing claims.

This model is applied as an editorial method without naming, quoting, or reproducing any external book.

## Consequence

The new system-manual PDFs supersede the English technical work reports as the primary external documentation. The previous PDFs remain historical references only.
