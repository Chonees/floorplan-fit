# Manual covers show invested days and compact stacks

## Decision

The first page of each final English system manual shows:

- the verified number of invested workdays, without hours;
- one compact `STACK:` line containing technology, language, framework, and library names separated by commas;
- no stack descriptions, categories, chronology, dates, or hour totals.

Verified cover totals:

- Point.ai: 31 days;
- FloorplanFit: 61 days.

## Retention

Only the two final system manuals remain in `output/pdf` as system documentation:

- `Point.ai-system-manual.pdf`;
- `FloorplanFit-system-manual.pdf`.

The earlier Spanish construction reports and English technical work reports are deleted rather than retained as historical external artifacts. Unrelated PDFs remain untouched.
